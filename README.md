# TRU WASH

FM 21-10 field hygiene. Hands before food and after the latrine. A cat hole is about 1 foot wide and 1 foot deep, covered at once. A latrine sits at least 100 feet from water, drains away from it, and is not dug into the water table. About 100 yards from the mess. No soap formula. Drinking water is TRU Water.
